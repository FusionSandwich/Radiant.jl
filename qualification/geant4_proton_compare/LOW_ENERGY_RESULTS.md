# Low-energy proton electronic-stopping benchmark (2026-09-25)

## Scope and independent reference

This extends the [matched Geant4/Radiant slab benchmark](RESULTS.md) from 10/100/499.99 MeV to **1.2, 2 and 5 MeV**. The reference method is the open-access [NIST PSTAR proton stopping and range program](https://www.nist.gov/pml/stopping-power-range-tables-electrons-protons-and-helium-ions), whose low-energy stopping fits draw on measurements and whose higher-energy model includes Bethe corrections. PSTAR is independent of Geant4's `hIoni` stopping calculation. We integrate PSTAR's **electronic** stopping along the same two one-dimensional characteristics as the matched slab test. This is a deterministic continuous-slowing-down approximation (CSDA) reference, **not** a second full irradiation/secondary-particle transport code. PSTAR's published CSDA range uses electronic **plus nuclear** stopping, so its range column is checked for data consistency but is not compared directly with the electronic-only layer deposits. [NIST defines these quantities and methods here](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Star/Text/programs.html).

The source remains uniformly distributed in Al, with energy uniform over `E ± 0.005 MeV` and directions `±1/√3` in x. It traverses an Al layer followed by a Cu layer; both layers have the same thickness in a given case. Material densities are the Geant4 case's Al `2.699` and Cu `8.96 g/cm³`. Slabs were thinned at low energy to keep every exiting characteristic **above Radiant's 1 MeV lower energy boundary**. Geant4 11.4.2 uses proton `G4hIonisation`, no loss fluctuations, no secondaries, no multiple scattering or hadronic reactions, and a 1 m secondary production cut. Each low-energy Geant4 case used 200,000 histories and seed `731293`; maximum steps were 10, 20 and 100 nm at 1.2, 2 and 5 MeV. The shared Geant4 stopping table still has SHA-256 `929e06363ccd6f702b95c09cb1354f096dfc150369fadd7ea0ec648929f0cfa9`.

NIST PSTAR's text interface returned 188 Al and 188 Cu points each, with a denser user grid near 1–20 MeV. The preserved CSV SHA-256 values are Al `5c4d8bb305421249251c8d125160875461ccd1f37f54e687a825769f6cd87bac` and Cu `a8024dbd2c5d0cf55dd60dddcb23b714c3fbfed2bf9f9de6f76c7b86e0a2cca0`. The derived MeV/cm electronic table, converted with the matched densities, has SHA-256 `c9f5801a6080c69ae0b60953c4fcfa9ca11d3be85fe88e8d444ef6c9b064fc9`. [Raw NIST responses](pstar_Al_response.html) and [conversion code](prepare_pstar_table.py) are retained.

## Layer deposition

Values are mean **MeV deposited per source proton** in each material. Radiant uses its native S2 SN/CSD solver and the same mass-score conversion documented in the original benchmark. Its selected grid is 64 voxels/material × 400 lower energy groups at 1.2 and 5 MeV, and 32 × 200 at 2 MeV. `G4 characteristic` integrates the *same Geant4 stopping table* independently of either transport solver; `PSTAR CSDA` instead uses independent NIST stopping input. Geant4 uncertainties are Monte Carlo standard errors only. [All values and relative differences](low_energy_summary.csv) are machine readable.

The selected Radiant/G4-table scores differ from Geant4 by at most **0.355%** (1.2 MeV Cu), from the shared-table characteristic solution by at most **0.187%**, and from independent PSTAR CSDA by at most **0.319%**. The Geant4 stopping coefficient itself differs from PSTAR by at most **0.291%** at these three incident energies in Al/Cu. Radiant with the PSTAR table differs from its own PSTAR characteristic reference by at most **0.176%**. These are comparisons between models; their small numerical differences do not establish sub-percent accuracy against experiment.

## Convergence and diagnostic checks

- Halving Geant4's max step at 1.2 and 2 MeV changed the Al/Cu means by at most `0.0112%`; the raw [full-step](geant4_low_1p2.csv) and [half-step](geant4_low_1p2_halfstep.csv) scores are preserved for both energies.
- Radiant's 16 × 100, 32 × 200, and where run 64 × 400 grid results are in [the grid table](radiant_low_energy_scores.csv). The largest 16 × 100 to selected-grid shift is `0.215%` (5 MeV Cu). At 1.2 MeV, changing the lower boundary from 1.0 to 1.1 MeV at 64 × 400 changed the Cu score by about `0.0052%`; the shared-table characteristic residual remains about `0.187%` and should be treated as a measured numerical/model discrepancy.
- Doubling the source quadrature from 64 × 8 to 128 × 16 and halving the path step changed PSTAR layer deposits by at most `4.3×10⁻⁶%`. The corresponding shared-Geant4-table characteristic values were stable to much better than `0.01%`.
- Integrating `1/(electronic+nuclear mass stopping)` from PSTAR's parsed table reproduces its tabulated CSDA **range increments** over 1–2, 2–5 and 5–10 MeV within `0.083%` in both materials. This checks parsing and units; nuclear stopping was still omitted from the matched layer runs.
- [The automated exploratory receipt](low_energy_validation_receipt.json) reports **58/58 checks passed** across domain, step, quadrature, data, and score comparisons. Its tolerances are screening criteria selected for this benchmark, not a preregistered experimental validation criterion. Recreate it with `python qualification/geant4_proton_compare/validate_low_energy.py`.

Radiant's inherited analytic `bethe` method was also evaluated directly against PSTAR; it is **not** the stopping input of the layer comparison. [The screen](bethe_vs_pstar_stopping.csv) finds Cu stopping `2.26%` below PSTAR at 1 MeV, `1.50%` below at 1.2 MeV, `0.60%` below at 2 MeV, and `0.19%` above at 5 MeV. NIST quotes larger low-energy stopping uncertainties (roughly 2–5% around 1 MeV), so this is a model difference to track rather than a definitive error assignment. It does preclude claiming a sub-percent check of Radiant's inherited Bethe model at the lowest energies. [NIST's uncertainty discussion](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Star/Text/programs.html) provides context.

## What this supports and what remains

For **thin elemental Al/Cu slabs above 1 MeV, with only proton electronic stopping active**, these tests support a bounded claim that Radiant's native source, stopping-table binding, S2/CSD transport and layer energy-deposition score agree with matched Geant4 and an independent PSTAR-derived deterministic CSDA reference to roughly `0.4%` for the tested observables. The 1.2 MeV Cu characteristic residual is retained in that bound. The independent PSTAR input also runs through the same Radiant binding at all three energies.

This does **not** validate full proton irradiation or DPA prediction: no nuclear stopping/recoil dose, nonelastic reactions, secondary particle fields, straggling, multiple scattering, projected range, thick-target Bragg peak, HTS compound, or experimental measurement was included. The current physical-mode guard remains blocked. A credible **full-model** claim needs those channels, exact-material data, depth/angle/secondary observables and an independent full-physics reference or measurement. At 10 MeV the PSTAR characteristic for the original 0.01 cm slabs predicts a minimum exit of `0.959 MeV`, below the 1 MeV contract; that case is reported in the exploratory [PSTAR comparison](pstar_comparison_128x16.csv) but excluded from the in-domain low-energy acceptance receipt.

## Reproduce locally

The environment and acquisition gate are in [LOW_ENERGY_ACQUISITION.md](LOW_ENERGY_ACQUISITION.md). `build_local.sh` uses the existing Geant4 11.4.2 WSL environment and existing build directory. `run_low_energy.sh` and `run_low_step_check.sh` reproduce the Geant4 cases; [source](proton_slab.cc) records the physics and source. `fetch_pstar.py` posts to NIST's official text endpoint, retains the raw responses and CSVs, and `prepare_pstar_table.py` converts electronic stopping to MeV/cm. `pstar_reference.py`, `g4_characteristic_low.py`, `check_pstar_range.py`, `summarize_low_energy.py`, and `validate_low_energy.py` regenerate the comparisons. `run_radiant.jl` accepts energy, layer thickness, output path and optional lower energy boundary arguments. Radiant runs used the existing Julia 1.10.12 manifest and depot recorded in the acquisition note; their [raw logs](radiant_low_1p2_64x400.log) and scores are preserved. No package was installed and no remote compute was used.
